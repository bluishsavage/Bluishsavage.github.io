The Creator Behind the Content

More Than Just Another Gaming Stream.

My name is Gavin Cloyd, but most people know me online as BluishSavage. I'm a gaming content creator and the owner of BluishSavage Media LLC.

My primary focus is Rainbow Six Siege, but I want to take the game beyond the usual desk-and-monitor setup. That means building portable gaming setups, traveling to interesting locations, meeting new people, and creating experiences that bring gaming into the real world.

From streaming in unique outdoor locations to visiting local businesses and bringing their experiences to a live audience, the goal is to make every stream something worth watching.

Featured Project

50 STATES.
50 SIEGE SESSIONS.

One game. One country. One ridiculous road trip.

The mission is to travel across all 50 states and play Rainbow Six Siege in every single one. Along the way, I'll explore unique locations, connect with local gaming communities, and discover what each state has to offer.

Featured Project

THE ELEVATION
CHALLENGE.

How high can you take a gaming setup? This project explores the limits of livestreaming by taking Rainbow Six Siege to progressively higher elevations.

From low-elevation locations to extraordinary mountain environments, the challenge is to bring a functional gaming setup to places you wouldn't normally expect to find one.
