Partnership Details

A few things to keep in mind when planning a promotional event.

Travel & Expenses

Travel, lodging, and other out-of-pocket expenses will be discussed and agreed upon when preparing your quote.

Location Requirements

The business and creator will coordinate suitable access to the location, electrical power, and internet before the event.

Performance Expectations

Specific viewer counts, impressions, engagement, and sales cannot be guaranteed. Available performance metrics can be provided after the campaign.

Content Ownership

Content ownership and any business reuse, paid advertising, or licensing rights will be agreed upon separately.

Content Approval

Business information and promotional claims can be reviewed for accuracy. Any additional approval requirements will be agreed upon in advance.

Scheduling

All partnerships are subject to availability, location suitability, and a mutually agreed schedule.
